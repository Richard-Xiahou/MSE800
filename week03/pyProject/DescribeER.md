This ER diagram is about a simple college management system. 
It has students, lecturers, subjects, lectures and enrolment information (Entities). 
A student can enrol in a course, lecturers teach subjects, and lectures have a date and time. 
The system helps the college store information about students and classes.

The database keeps important information such as student detail (attributes), lecturer details, subject information and enrolment records. It can make it easier to manage courses and find student information.

circle shape represents an attributes.
rectangle shape represents an weak entity.
damond shape represents an strong entity.
